# TechCrunch — AllSpice $6M Round

> The commercial counterweight to [[ondsel-shutdown-announcement]]: the same idea, aimed at
> electronics and at enterprises, did raise and did grow.

## Summary

TechCrunch reports AllSpice closing a **$6M** round in late summer 2023 from existing seed
investors (Root Ventures, Flybridge, Bowery Capital, Benchstrength, and angels), following
a **$3.2M** seed in 2022. Co-founders are Valentina Ratner and Kyle Dumont.

The reported story is a deliberate pivot from SMB to enterprise after finding large
organizations had the same collaboration problems. Dumont attributes timing to "companies
adapting to new market conditions that included downsizing and layoffs, meaning doing more
with fewer people." Reported traction: 10x growth in users and revenue year over year, 100+
interactions per user per week, top cohorts at ~37 hours monthly in the platform, and
organic expansion where each engineer brings 2-3 collaborators from other teams within 4-6
months.
