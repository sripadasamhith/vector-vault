## Why they closed

Ondsel ceased operations after nearly two years, unable to reach commercial viability.
Despite surveying and interviewing roughly 100 engineers, the team found that "we failed
to find commercial adoption to justify a venture-capitalized startup." They saw hobbyist
enthusiasm but could not convert it into business-level traction.

## What they built

1. **Ondsel ES** — a distribution of FreeCAD with an improved UI, faster release cadence,
   and enhanced Sketcher and TechDraw workbenches.
2. **Lens** — a cloud collaboration platform for organizing hardware development
   workflows: sharing models with privacy controls (including PIN-protected links) and
   publishing parametric designs that could be modified online and downloaded.

## Market conclusions

The team's analysis was that proprietary CAD dominates through institutional
entrenchment — it is embedded in educational curricula and deeply rooted in industrial
workflows. The gap between enthusiast adoption and commercial demand proved insurmountable
for their business model. Their read: offering a better open-source alternative is not by
itself sufficient to displace established tools in professional settings.
