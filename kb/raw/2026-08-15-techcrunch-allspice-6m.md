## What AllSpice does

A Git-based developer platform for hardware engineers: component library management,
CI/CD ("Actions"), CAD file support, and supply chain component availability tracking.

## Funding

- **$6 million** round closed in late summer 2023.
- Investors: Root Ventures, Flybridge Capital Partners, Bowery Capital, Benchstrength, and
  an angel group — all existing seed-stage investors.
- Followed a **$3.2 million** seed round announced in 2022.

## Founders

Valentina Ratner and Kyle Dumont, co-founders.

## Strategic pivot

Initially targeted small businesses, then shifted to enterprise after finding large
organizations had identical collaboration problems. Dumont on the trigger: "companies
adapting to new market conditions that included downsizing and layoffs, meaning doing more
with fewer people."

## Traction claims (as of the article)

- User base and revenue grew **10x** over the prior year.
- Over **100 interactions per week per user** on average.
- Top user cohorts spend roughly **37 hours monthly** on the platform.
- Organic expansion: individual engineers recruit colleagues within 4-6 months; each
  engineer has **2-3 collaborators from other teams** using the platform.

## Problem framing

Hardware developers lacked collaborative development infrastructure comparable to GitHub
for software, creating friction around design iteration, component management, and
stakeholder communication.
